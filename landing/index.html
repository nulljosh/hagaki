Mailbag

Inbox zero.
One button.

Mailbag reads your Gmail or iCloud inbox and sorts it. Machine mail goes into seven folders. People go to your archive. Press Clear inbox and it is empty. Nothing is deleted.

Try the demo

Web, iPhone and Mac.

Your inbox

One button

Clear inbox files the machine mail and archives the rest. Your inbox is empty when it finishes. File everything is the gentler one: it leaves mail from people where it is.

Seven folders

Receipts, travel, dev, newsletters, social, promotions and junk. Each one is a real label in Gmail or a real folder in iCloud, under a folder called Mailbag.

Sorted twice

Plain rules go first. They know a receipt, a build alert and a boarding pass. A small AI model reads the sender and subject of what is left and catches cold pitches and promos the rules miss.

Nothing is deleted

Filing moves mail out of your inbox and into its folder. Junk gets a folder too. Delete is a separate button, and in Gmail it goes to Trash, not the void.

People are safe

Mail from a real person never goes to a folder. It stays in your inbox, or goes to your archive when you clear. A marketplace buyer or a collections notice is never left to the AI.

Look first

The list is a preview. Nothing moves until you press a button. Unsubscribe is one click and sends the sender's own one-click request where they support it.
